About

I'm passionate data scientist and software engineer with a deep interest in urban mobility and smart cities. My research and work span various domains, including data analysis, machine learning, IoT, Multi-agent Systems, Mobility Simulation and transportation systems.

Currently I'm a researcher at the Transportation Research Institute (IMOB) UHasselt Belgium working on my doctoral thesis in drone’s collaboration for smart cities applications. I'm a member of the Transportation Behavior research group. I was a research assistant at German University of Technology in Oman. I hold a master degree in Statistics and Data Science from Data Science Institute (DSI), UHasselt , Belgium and a software engineer degree from Higher Institute of Applied Sciences and Technologies, Tunisia. My research interests include Data Analysis, Machine learning, Artificial Intelligence (AI) Multi-Agent Systems, Self-Organization and Drone Collaboration, Transportation, IoT and Smart Cities.
